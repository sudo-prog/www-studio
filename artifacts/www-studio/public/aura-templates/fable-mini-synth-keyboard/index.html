Drag knobs vertically (or use the arrow keys when focused). Hold several keys with the arpeggiator on and the MS-2 will step through them; Z–M and Q–I play the two octaves, −/+ shift the range. The signal path is oscillator → ADSR amplifier → resonant low-pass → master, with a single LFO routed to pitch or filter cutoff.
