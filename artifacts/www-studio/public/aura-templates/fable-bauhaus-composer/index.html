1919–1933 · Fourteen years of form follows function

Circle, square, triangle. Red, blue, yellow. Kandinsky asked students to match each shape to its natural color; the school never quite agreed on the answer. This composer takes the same vocabulary and lets a seeded algorithm settle the argument differently every time.
