Three ideas behind Lumen

Everything else is an implementation detail. These are the promises we design against.

Sense

Circadian by default

Lumen tracks the sun for your exact latitude and shifts every fixture from crisp 5 600 K mornings to a 1 900 K candle glow, in steps too small to notice and too consistent to ignore.

Compose

Scenes that blend

Scenes are not presets, they are gradients in time. Cross-fade from "Focus" to "Dinner" over twenty minutes, or snap instantly when the doorbell rings. Every transition is eased, never stepped.

Trust

Local, silent, yours

The whole OS runs on a hub the size of a coaster. No cloud round-trips, no account required, and the scene engine keeps working when the internet does not.
