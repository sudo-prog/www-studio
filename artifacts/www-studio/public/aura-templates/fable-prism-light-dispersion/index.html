Why the rainbow
lives inside glass

White light is every colour at once. Glass slows violet a little more than red, so each colour turns by a different angle and the beam unfolds into a spectrum.

About dispersion

About dispersion

At each surface the beam obeys Snell's law. The refractive index of glass is not a single number: it rises toward the blue end of the spectrum (Cauchy's approximation), so blue light is bent through a larger angle than red.

n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂  ·  n(λ) ≈ A + B/λ²
