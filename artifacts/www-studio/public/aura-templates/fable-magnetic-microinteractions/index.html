Microinteraction study · No. 054

Small joys,
ten of them.

Interfaces are remembered for their smallest moments: the way a switch lands, the pause before a checkmark. Each card below is one such moment, built by hand with spring physics and system fonts only.

01

Magnetic button

Pulls toward the cursor within a radius; the label drifts a little further than the body.

02

Liquid toggle

The knob stretches like a droplet mid-travel and overshoots before settling.

03

Drawn checkmark

The tick is a stroked path revealed by dash-offset; the box tilts as it fills.

04
