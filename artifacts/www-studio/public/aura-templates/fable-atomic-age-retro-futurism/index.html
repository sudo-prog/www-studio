Amenities of the Space Age

Kidney Pool

Solar-warmed, chlorine-fresh, and shaped like a comet's tail. Diving board tuned to a perfect C-sharp.

Color Television

Every room. Three channels in living RCA color, plus a fourth that only shows static from Alpha Centauri.

Sputnik Lounge

Atomic cocktails served in orbit-shaped glassware. Try the Tang Sour — it's exactly what the astronauts don't drink.

Roof Observatory

A twelve-inch refractor under a rotating dome. Nightly star talks by our resident astronomer, Miss Lorraine Vega.

Rocket Wake-Up

A gentle countdown from ten replaces the alarm. Room service arrives at T-minus zero with coffee and a frosted Danish.

Carport Charging

Pull your Bel Air under the fins. Complimentary windshield wash and a free tank of optimism with every stay.

Tonight on the roof

The Observatory Program

Our dome opens each evening at dusk. Bring a sweater; the desert cools quickly, and Saturn is worth a shiver. Guests receive a hand-stamped star chart with the coordinates of everything they saw — and one thing they didn't.
